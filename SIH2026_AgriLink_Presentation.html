Ground Realities & Pain Points

The Challenge: Agricultural Market Inefficiencies in India

🔴 Information Asymmetry
  • Smallholders lack real-time visibility into price fluctuations across nearby Mandis and corporate buyers.
  • Price discovery is dominated by unorganized middlemen taking 15-30% cuts.
  • Farmers lack forward-looking insights to decide optimal sale windows (Hold vs Sell).
🔴 Distress Sales & Losses
  • Immediate liquidity needs force farmers into distress sales immediately after harvest.
  • Lack of transparent logistics and cold storage causes 20-25% post-harvest spoilage.
  • Small lot sizes prevent individual farmers from negotiating with bulk processors.
🔴 Buyer Aggregation Risks
  • Institutional buyers struggle to aggregate consistent volumes across scattered smallholders.
  • Quality specs (moisture, grading) are non-standardized.
  • High risk of contract defaults, payment disputes, and delayed settlements.

Trust & Settlement Engine

Guaranteed Payment: 7-Stage Escrow Lifecycle

1. INITIATED Bid accepted by seller. Legal trade contract created.
2. ESCROW DEPOSITED Buyer locks 100% payment into secure escrow gateway.
3. DISPATCHED Produce packed and dispatched with tracking number.
4. DELIVERED Goods arrive at buyer warehouse for inspection.
5. ESCROW RELEASED

Buyer approves physical quality. Escrow instantly dispatches funds to farmer bank account; marks produce lot as SOLD.

6. DISPUTE / PAUSE

If moisture or quality variance occurs, escrow auto-pauses until administrative mediation reaches resolution.

7. CANCELLED / REVERT

If trade is voided, funds are refunded and produce lot immediately reverts back to AVAILABLE status.
