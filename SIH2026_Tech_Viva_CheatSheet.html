🔄 3. How It Works (The 7-Stage Escrow Lifecycle)
🌾 Farmer & Buyer Journey
1. Registration & KYC: Farmer registers with mobile OTP, village, and bank details.
2. Digital Lot Creation: Farmer lists crop, quantity (quintals), quality grade (Agmark A/B/C), and moisture %.
3. Smart Matchmaking: AI matches crop with institutional buyers (FMCG, processors, exporters).
4. Bidding & Offer: Buyer places competitive digital bid or counter-offer.
🔒 7-Stage Secure Escrow Flow
1. Initiated: Farmer accepts buyer's bid; contract locks digitally.
2. Escrow Deposited: Buyer deposits 100% money upfront into escrow.
3. Dispatched: Farmer ships produce with tracking reference.
4. Delivered: Goods arrive at buyer warehouse for inspection.
5. Escrow Released: Buyer verifies quality → funds sent to farmer instantly.
6. Dispute / Mediation: Paused if quality mismatch occurs; admin resolves.
❓ 4. Top Technical Questions Judges Will Ask & Winning Answers
📌 Q1: Why did you choose FastAPI over Flask or Django?
Answer: "We chose FastAPI because it is natively asynchronous (ASGI), which allows it to handle thousands of concurrent requests with extremely low latency. It is significantly faster than Flask and Django, provides automatic data validation via Pydantic, and generates interactive Swagger/OpenAPI documentation automatically."
📌 Q2: Where does the live mandi price data come from?
Answer: "We integrate directly with official Government open datasets and APIs — specifically e-NAM (National Agriculture Market) connecting 1,361+ mandis and Agmarknet (Directorate of Marketing & Inspection, GoI) daily modal price feeds on data.gov.in."
📌 Q3: How does the AI Price Forecasting work?
Answer: "We use a hybrid time-series model (ARIMA + LSTM) trained on historical mandi rates and arrival volumes. It predicts expected price ranges for the next 7 days and generates an intelligent 'HOLD or SELL' advisory so farmers avoid distress sales during seasonal glut troughs."
📌 Q4: How do you guarantee the farmer gets paid safely?
Answer: "Through our 7-stage Escrow Lifecycle. Once a bid is finalized, the buyer must deposit 100% of the funds into a secure escrow account before the farmer dispatches the crop. Once the buyer receives and approves the quality, the funds are automatically released to the farmer's bank account."
📌 Q5: How will uneducated or rural farmers use this app?
Answer: "We designed FairCrop with: (1) Full support for 22 official Indian languages in native scripts, (2) Voice search input in regional dialects, (3) High-contrast visual icons and simplified cards, and (4) FPO (Farmer Producer Organization) collective onboarding, where FPO leaders create bulk lots on behalf of village members."
📌 Q6: What if internet connectivity is weak in the village?
Answer: "We built the client using lightweight, responsive Vanilla JS and offline-first LocalStorage caching. Critical prices and lot details load instantly even on 2G/3G speeds without heavy framework bloat."
📌 Q7: What is the Business & Revenue Model?
Answer: "FairCrop is 100% FREE for farmers. We generate revenue by charging a nominal 0.5%–1.5% convenience fee to large institutional buyers (food processors, retailers, exporters) for verified quality assaying, consolidated FPO sourcing, and end-to-end logistics coordination."
📌 Q8: How is FairCrop different from e-NAM or existing apps?
Answer: "While e-NAM focuses on mandi-level physical auctions, FairCrop enables direct farm-gate procurement with AI predictive sell advisories, automated buyer-seller matchmaking, and guaranteed Escrow protection, eliminating traditional mandi transportation hassles and commission agent fees."
